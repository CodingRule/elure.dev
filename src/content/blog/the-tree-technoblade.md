# The Tree

There is something strangely beautiful about hearing an absurd moment inside a
Minecraft game turn into a meditation on identity, isolation, free will, and
hope.

What begins with a man trapped inside a tree gradually becomes something that
reads almost like existential prose poetry-while still ending in the most
Technoblade way possible.

> “I’ve begun to question: Why am I here? What is my purpose inside this tree?
> Was I placed here by some greater power, or... was it my destiny to be trapped
> inside this tree? Do I truly have free will?
>
> I ponder these questions as life slowly goes on without me. Is this a dream?
> W-was the life I knew before even real? I call for help, but no one answers. I
> begin to question whether there was ever a life outside...
>
> The tree... Who am I? Am I the tree?
>
> During these past two years, I’ve had a lot of time to think. I’ve learned that
> the tree loves me, and I love the tree. The tree protects me-and one day, I
> shall be free.
>
> I... I dream of that day. The day that... I am awakened.
>
> I hear a faint sound. Is it lightning? But if lightning exists, then everything
> else must exist, too. I feel a pull. It is time to leave the tree.
>
> I’m apprehensive, yet eager to return to the outside world-and **I still won**.”
>
> - Technoblade, *The Tree*

It is funny, dramatic, unexpectedly sincere, and somehow genuinely moving. For
a moment, the tree is no longer merely a glitch or an obstacle. It becomes a
prison, a protector, and eventually something that must be left behind.

That is probably why the monologue stayed with me: beneath the joke is the
simple hope that, however long we feel stuck, one day something will pull us
forward-and we will be ready to leave.
